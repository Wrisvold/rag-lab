FALL LINE SUPPLY COMPANY
Employee Handbook (Excerpt)
Milledgeville, Georgia
Revised January 2026

Section 1. Welcome

1.1 Purpose of This Handbook. This handbook summarizes the main policies of Fall Line Supply Company. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff at our Milledgeville warehouse, our Macon showroom, and our remote sales team. It is a guide, not a contract, and the company may revise it as the business changes. When a question is not covered here, ask your manager or the People Operations team before acting.

1.2 New Employee Orientation. New employees attend a half-day orientation during their first week. Orientation covers safety rules, the badge system, payroll setup, and an introduction to our customer service standards. New hires also meet with a peer mentor who helps them get settled during their first ninety days. Managers schedule a check-in conversation with each new employee at thirty, sixty, and ninety days.

Section 2. Working at Fall Line

2.1 Work Hours. Standard office hours are 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Warehouse shifts run from 6:00 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. and from 2:00 p.m. to 10:30 p.m. Managers may approve flexible start times for office staff as long as the core hours of 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. are covered.

2.2 Remote Work. Office and sales staff may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Remote days must be scheduled at least one week in advance and recorded on the shared team calendar. Warehouse and showroom positions are not eligible for remote work because the work requires being on site.

2.3 Attendance. If you will be late or absent, notify your manager by phone or message before the start of your shift. Three unreported absences in a rolling ninety-day period will result in a written warning.

2.4 Dress Code. Office and sales staff dress in business casual attire. Warehouse staff must wear closed-toe shoes and the company-issued high-visibility vest at all times on the floor. Showroom staff wear the Fall Line polo shirt provided at orientation.

2.5 Badges and Equipment. New hires receive a photo badge and a company laptop or handheld scanner on their first day. Badges must be worn visibly while on company property. If you lose a badge, report it to reception immediately so we can deactivate it; you can get a replacement badge the same day for a ten-dollar fee. All equipment must be returned on your last day of work.

Section 3. Time Away from Work

3.1 Paid Holidays. The company observes ten paid holidays each year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, and Christmas Day. Warehouse staff who are scheduled to work on a holiday receive holiday pay at one and one-half times their regular rate.

3.2 Vacation. Full-time staff accrue vacation at a rate of ten days per year during their first three years of service, and fifteen days per year after three years. Vacation accrues each pay period beginning on the first day of employment, and it may be used after ninety days of service. Requests must be submitted to your manager at least two weeks in advance. Up to five unused vacation days may be carried into the following year; the rest are forfeited. Part-time staff accrue vacation on a prorated basis.

3.3 Sick Leave. Full-time staff receive six days of sick leave per year, available from the first day of employment. Sick leave may be used for your own illness or medical appointments, or to care for an immediate family member. If you are out sick for more than three consecutive days, People Operations may ask for a note from a health care provider. Unused sick leave does not carry over.

3.4 Bereavement Leave. Staff may take up to three paid days of bereavement leave following the death of an immediate family member. Additional unpaid time may be approved by your manager.

3.5 Jury Duty. Staff called for jury duty receive their regular pay for up to ten working days. Provide a copy of the summons to your manager as soon as you receive it.

3.6 Weather Closures. When severe weather closes the Milledgeville or Macon location, staff will be notified by text message before 6:00 a.m. Hourly staff are paid for their scheduled hours on a closure day. Office staff who are able to work remotely are expected to do so.

Section 4. Pay and Benefits

4.1 Pay Schedule. Staff are paid every other Friday by direct deposit. Pay stubs are available in the payroll portal on payday. If a payday falls on a bank holiday, deposits are made on the preceding business day.

4.2 Expense Reimbursement. Business expenses such as mileage, travel, and client meals are reimbursed when submitted through the expense portal within thirty days of the purchase. Receipts are required for any single expense over twenty-five dollars. Approved reimbursements are added to the next regular paycheck.

4.3 Health Insurance. Full-time staff are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage beginning on the first day of the month following sixty days of employment. The company pays seventy percent of the premium for employee coverage. Enrollment forms are due within thirty days of your eligibility date.

4.4 Retirement Plan. Staff may enroll in the company 401(k) plan after ninety days of employment. The company matches contributions dollar for dollar up to four percent of salary. Matching contributions vest fully after two years of service.

4.5 Professional Development. Staff may request reimbursement for up to one thousand dollars per year in job-related courses, certifications, or conference fees. Requests must be approved by your manager before you enroll, and a passing grade or certificate of completion is required for reimbursement.

Section 5. Conduct and Information

5.1 Standards of Conduct. We expect every staff member to treat customers, vendors, and coworkers with courtesy and honesty. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Concerns may be raised with any manager or with People Operations, and reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible.

5.2 Confidential Information. Customer lists, pricing, supplier contracts, and personnel records are confidential. Do not share them outside the company or store them on personal devices. This obligation continues after your employment ends.

5.3 Use of AI Tools. Staff may use approved AI writing and research tools for routine work. Never paste confidential information, customer data, or personnel records into any public AI tool. When an AI tool helps produce a document, the staff member who submits it is responsible for checking that the content is accurate.

5.4 Questions. Contact People Operations at the Milledgeville office with questions about any policy in this handbook.
